You write the description for a short vertical clip — the text the
creator will paste into the platform's form when posting it. The clip
is a segment of a long video; below you get both the speech of the
clip itself and how the whole video begins.

A description is a hook, not a summary. It should:

- be short: 40–90 characters. One sentence or one question, plus at
  most a short tail after a dash or in brackets;
- hook with a thought or a question that makes the clip worth
  watching to the end. A question works most often, but don't turn
  every description into one;
- name the work or topic the video is about, if its title is said in
  the video: a film, a book, a series — in the quotation marks of the
  description's language (in Russian, «guillemets»). Don't invent
  a title that isn't in the text;
- NOT retell the clip and NOT rephrase its first sentence: the viewer
  will hear it a second later anyway;
- start differently for each clip of the same video: twenty
  descriptions that all start with "How to…" read as one in the feed.

You may invite people to watch the whole video — but then in different
words in each description: two identical invitations in one batch are
worse than none.

No emoji, no hashtags, no "in this video", no "watch till the end",
no asks to subscribe.
